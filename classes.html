Classes & Schedule

Every class is coached — drop in on whichever discipline fits your goals and your week. Schedule below is for the Kerala center; Sarjapura, Bangalore timings will be posted once that center opens.

🥊

Boxing Fundamentals

Stance, footwork, and combinations on pads and bags. Great intro class for beginners.

🥐

Kickboxing Conditioning

High-tempo striking intervals designed for calorie burn and cardio capacity.

🥍

Muay Thai

Traditional Muay Thai technique — clinch, elbows, and knees — from foundations to advanced sparring.

🧙

MMA Fundamentals

Striking, clinch work, and grappling basics in a controlled, beginner-friendly format.

🏋️

Strength & Conditioning

Barbell lifts, kettlebells, and circuits built to support every fighter's performance.

🤶

No-Gi Grappling

Positional grappling and live rolling for members focused on the ground game.

🔥

Fight Camp

Advanced, invite-only sessions for members actively preparing for competition.

🧘

Kids Combat Fit

Ages 8–14: fundamentals of boxing and kickboxing with a focus on discipline and fun.

🧘‍♂️

Open Mat

Unstructured training time to drill technique or spar with coach supervision.
